# Reading the editorial policy

What `findopera policy` prints, whole or a piece at a time. The answer in each
case is a cut-down policy with the real one's shape: a procedure is a
paragraph opening `**P1.`, a stage a `## Stage N:` heading, a rule a `### R4.`
heading, and a `---` between stages that belongs to neither.

That last is the reason these exist. The `---` sits under the last rule of a
stage, and a reader asking for that rule should not be handed it.
