A group is dropped when a placeholder at its own level turns out to be absent.
One inside a nested group drops that group instead, so it cannot keep the
outer one alive — the outer brackets here would always render.

--- template
[ - [{{year}}]]
--- expect
error[template_empty_group]: this group contains no placeholder of its own, so it would always render
[ - [{{year}}]]
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
help: A group `[…]` is dropped when a placeholder inside it turns out to be absent. For a literal bracket, write `\[` and `\]`.
