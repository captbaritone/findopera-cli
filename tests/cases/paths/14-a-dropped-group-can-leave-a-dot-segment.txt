A literal `.` segment is caught at parse time, but one that only appears
because a group dropped cannot be — which is why the check survives in
`to_path` as well.

--- template
{{composer.lastName}}/[{{year}}]./{{opera.title}}
--- data
composer.lastName = Strauss
opera.title = Salome
--- error
error[path_traversal]: rendered "Strauss/./Salome", which contains a `.` or `..` segment
